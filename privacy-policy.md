# Privacy Policy for Postwise

**Effective date:** 3 October 2026

Postwise ("the app", "we", "us") is a content planner for short videos,
developed and published by **HILAL AHMAD HUSSAIN** ("the developer"). This
policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes and the choices you
have. It applies to the Postwise app on Android and iOS.

If you have a question about this policy, contact **tolloop1999@gmail.com**.

## 1. The short version

- **Your plans stay on your phone.** Your accounts, videos, captions, scripts,
  ideas, schedules and stats are stored only on your device. We do not run a
  server, and we never receive or upload this content.
- **No sign-up.** Postwise has no user accounts and never asks for the password
  of TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or any other service.
- **Ads and analytics use Google services.** The app shows ads through Google
  AdMob and measures app usage through Google Firebase Analytics. These services
  collect some device and usage information, described below.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see
or access it:

- Profiles you add (name, handle, platform, optional profile picture)
- Content plans: video references, captions, hashtags, scripts, notes,
  checklists, series, sounds, ideas, templates and posting times
- Stats you type in by hand (views, likes and similar numbers)
- Goals, streaks and app settings
- The app-lock PIN, stored only as a salted, one-way hash

**Videos and photos.** When you pick a video or image, the app saves a reference
to the file in your gallery. Your originals are never copied to us, uploaded or
deleted by the app.

**Backups.** If you export a backup, the app creates a file and you choose where
to save or share it. Where that file goes afterwards (for example a cloud drive
or a chat) is up to you and that service's own privacy policy. Your app-lock PIN
is not included in backups.

**Deleting your data.** Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all
of this data from your device. We keep no copy, so we cannot restore it.

## 3. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the services below. They are operated by Google, which processes
the data under its own privacy policy.

### Google Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand how many people use the app and which
features they use, so we can improve it. It may collect:

- An app instance ID (a random identifier for this installation)
- Device model, operating system version, language and app version
- Approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address)
- Usage events, such as app opens, sessions, screens viewed and time spent

Analytics does **not** receive your captions, scripts, videos, profile names or
any other content you create in the app.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version of Postwise shows a banner ad. AdMob may collect:

- Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where
  permitted)
- IP address, device and network information
- Information about ad views and taps

AdMob may use this information to show ads, measure them and, where you allow
it, personalise them.

**Consent.** In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and
other regions where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form
before personalised ads are served. You can change your choice at any time in
**Settings > Privacy & security > Ad privacy**.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised
ads, in your device settings (Android: *Settings > Google > Ads*; iOS:
*Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking*).

## 4. Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when the related feature needs it. You can
refuse or withdraw any permission in your device settings, and the rest of the
app keeps working.

| Permission | Why the app uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Photos and videos | To let you pick videos and images for your plans and preview them. Files are read on the device only. |
| Notifications, alarms and vibration | To remind you when it is time to post. |
| Start at boot | To restore your reminders after the phone restarts. |
| Calendar (optional) | Only if you turn on calendar sync: to add your posting times to a calendar on your device. If that calendar is synced to an online account (for example Google Calendar), your calendar provider will sync those events. |
| Fingerprint or face unlock (optional) | Only if you turn on app lock. The check is done by your phone's operating system; the app never receives your fingerprint or face data. |
| Internet | Used only by the ad and analytics services. Planning features work offline. |

## 5. What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not post to TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or any other platform
  for you. You publish your content yourself in those apps.
- We do not access your social media accounts, messages or contacts.

## 6. Children

Postwise is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in
your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it
where we are able to.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California
Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or
object to the processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent and
to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because your planner data never leaves your device, you control it directly in
the app. For data processed by Google's ad and analytics services, you can use
the ad privacy settings above, reset your advertising ID, or contact us and we
will help with your request.

**Legal bases (EU/UK users).** Your planner data is processed on your device to
provide the app you asked for. Analytics is processed on the basis of our
legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. Personalised
ads are served only with your consent.

## 8. Data security and retention

Your planner data is protected by your device's security and, if you turn it on,
by the app lock. Data held by Google services is kept according to Google's
retention settings and policies. No method of storage or transmission is
completely secure, but we choose services and settings that protect your
information.

## 9. International transfers

Google may process analytics and advertising data on servers outside your
country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the European
Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for
important changes, let you know in the app or on the store listing. Continuing
to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
